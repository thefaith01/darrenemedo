# Product: darrenemedo.com

## Register

Personal professional site. Reading and persuading, not operating.

## Users

- Leaseholders, residents' management companies and RTM directors deciding whether to join the waiting list.
- Property industry peers, recruiters and mentees checking credentials and track record.
- Readers of In Progress and He Who Has Ears looking for the writing.

Most arrive from LinkedIn or Google on a phone.

## Purpose

Show who Darren is, what he has delivered, and what clients say, then make it easy to join the waiting list, read the writing, or get in touch.

## Principles

- Calm and credible. The tone of a chartered professional, not a startup.
- Evidence over claims. Real reviews, real outcomes, plain language.
- Darren's own words stay his. Copy edits are proposed, never silent.
- Readable first: 17px body, short line lengths, strong contrast.

## Accessibility

- WCAG AA contrast for all text.
- Every section readable without JavaScript.
- Respect reduced motion.
